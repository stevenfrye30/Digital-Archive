v119 Roer), the principal-Upanishad layer is essentially mature.

Aiyar-primary `MUKTIKA_108` and produce a disciplined acquisition
roadmap — what can be done now, what is blocked, what must wait for
public-domain dates, and what should be searched next.

## 3. Source-family roadmap

### A. Adyar Library English Series (1935–1978)

The Adyar Library at Adyar (Theosophical Society headquarters,
Chennai) published the canonical English translations of the
sectarian Upanishads in a sequence of volumes that the Hume 1921
bibliography couldn't catalogue (they hadn't been published yet).
These are the **dominant unrealised batch** for the missing 64.

## 4. Immediate-source audit (v120 attempts)

In the spirit of *"do not force ingestion, but ingest if a safe
source is already available"*, v120 ran 2 targeted IA queries:

1. **Multi-key search**: `Nrisimha OR Nrisimhatapani OR Mahanarayana OR Atharvasiras English translation date:[1850 TO 1928]`
   — 7.3M-document IA index returned mostly unrelated results; no
   matching Upanishad translation surfaced.
2. **Tattvabhushan**: `Tattvabhushan Upanishads`
   — Returned only Tattvabhushan's *Hindu Theism* (1898) and his
   1921 *Lectures on the Theism of the Upanishads* (commentary
   essays, not translations). The 3-volume Calcutta Upanishads
   edition (1900–1904) Hume's bibliography catalogued is **not
   findable on IA via JSON API**.

Conclusion: **no immediate ingestion is safe**. v120 is a
strategy-report-only pass.
