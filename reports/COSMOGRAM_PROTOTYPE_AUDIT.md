## The Cosmogram

The cosmology AO chamber (AO·001, gen1-cosmology-firmament)
now carries — in its `§ I — Diagram` section — a tall vertical
manuscript cosmogram, replacing the prior landscape SVG.

### Composition

- **ViewBox 480 × 880 — portrait.** The cosmogram is taller
  than it is wide. The reader's eye descends rather than
  scanning across.
- **Five named strata stacked vertically:**

| # | Stratum (Hebrew transliterated, small-caps italic) | English gloss | Symbolic mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | `MAYIM MĒʿAL LĀ-RĀQÎAʿ` | the upper cosmic waters | wavy water pattern band |
| II | `RĀQÎAʿ` | the firmament — sky-vault hammered out | curved dome + sun ○ + moon ☽ + five star ✦ glyphs set in it |
| III | `ʾEREṢ` | the earth — a disc resting on pillars | horizontal earth-line + 5 pillars descending into the abyss |
| IV | `MAYIM MITTAḤAT LĀ-RĀQÎAʿ` | the lower waters — tehôm, the deep | wavy water pattern band (denser) |
| V | `SHEOL` | the depths — the shadow-land | descending hairline into a threefold-dot mark `∴` |

- **A dashed cosmic-axis line** runs the full height (y=56 to
  y=836) at x=240 (center). Stroke 0.6, alpha 0.16, dash
  pattern `2 6` — read as continuity, not chrome. This is
  the axis mundi.
- **Inter-stratum centred dots** (`·`) mark the four pauses
  between strata along the cosmic axis.
- **Sepia line-and-type only.** No fills, no colors, no
  illustration of figures or scenes. Restrained ink-and-
  vellum register.

### Typography

The SVG carries four new typographic registers:
- `.cc-d-name` — the stratum name. Georgia italic small-caps,
  12.5 px, letter-spacing 0.18 em, fill `rgba(85, 50, 18,
  0.88)`. The named cosmic domain.
- `.cc-d-gloss` — the stratum gloss. Georgia italic, 10.5 px,
  fill `rgba(110, 90, 60, 0.74)`. The quiet English
  description.
- `.cc-d-mark` — inter-stratum dot. 11 px, alpha 0.42. The
  axis-mundi pause.
- `.cc-d-deep` — closing threefold-dot glyph. 12 px italic,
  alpha 0.6. The descent into the unfigured depths.

### Chrome

The previous chamber wrapped the diagram in a `<figure>` with
a parchment-gradient background + a 1 px border. That treated
the diagram as "an inset card in the chamber." For the
masterpiece prototype the wrapper is stripped:
- `background: transparent`
- `border: 0`
- `max-width: 480px`
- Centered

The cosmogram sits directly on the chamber's parchment as a
preserved figure, not as a card-inset.

## Why this works

A real medieval/Renaissance manuscript cosmogram (Hildegard
of Bingen, the Beatus manuscripts, the Liber Floridus, etc.)
shares three properties:

1. **Vertical descending architecture** — the cosmos is
   represented as a stack of named domains, top-to-bottom.
2. **Named layers in their native language** — each cosmic
   region carries its own canonical name in the manuscript's
   tradition.
3. **Restraint** — line art and labels, no decoration, the
   reader expected to know the cosmos and recognize the
   architecture.

The codex's cosmogram now has all three.

Earlier the chamber's existing SVG was landscape and small —
the cosmos read as a wide thin band glanced over. The reader
saw "a figure with labels." Now the cosmos reads as a tall
descending architecture — the reader's eye walks down through
it. The Hebrew names give each stratum native authority. The
gloss is quiet enough to support, not lecture. The cosmic
axis ties the composition into a single architectural whole.

The cosmogram is no longer a figure inside an essay. The
cosmogram IS the chamber's centerpiece artifact. Opening
AO·001 is encountering a preserved world-picture.

## What this pass does NOT do

Per the brief, explicitly:

- **No infographic aesthetics** — no charts, gauges, axes,
  legends, percentages, or quantified labels.
- **No educational diagrams** — no "key" boxes, no glossary
  hovers, no expandable details.
- **No interactive science graphics** — the SVG is static;
  no hover behaviors, no clicks.
- **No fantasy map energy** — no compass roses, no decorative
  borders, no themed icons.
- **No fills, no colors beyond the codex's sepia register.**
- **No new HTML class names beyond `cc-d-name`, `cc-d-gloss`,
  `cc-d-mark`, `cc-d-deep`** — extensions to the existing
  cc-diagram typographic family.

The composition remains: quiet, manuscriptal, civilizational,
inevitable.
