# Object Artifact Audit

> *Note (2026-07-27, Task 16): the steward renamed "Atlas Objects" to **Codex Objects** ("Atlas" now belongs solely to the workspace's graph-engine layer). This dated record keeps its original wording.*

*Compiled 2026-05-22, v83. The transition document from
codex-architecture passes (v76-v82) into reader-facing object
maturation. Companion to all prior constitutional documents.*

For eight passes the codex has built its constitutional
infrastructure: leaf material, folio architecture, reading
choreography, canonical orientation, witness temporality,
civilizational inheritance, civilizational geography, and the
witness-family register. Those systems are now stable.

The frontier moves. The next work is on *the artifacts the
reader encounters inside the codex* — each witness family
must evolve from "content in a styled leaf" into "a preserved
intellectual artifact." This document is the first audit of
that work: what each family IS, as an artifact species.

---

## Artifact taxonomy

For each family, what kind of preserved object does the
reader encounter?

### Commentary witnesses — *preserved interpretive folio*

The accumulated reading tradition: Augustine, Philo, Aquinas,
Barth, plus the patriarchal commentaries (lekh-lekha,
Jacob's ladder, the binding, etc.).

**Artifact logic.** A folio leaf bearing an interpreter's
sustained argument, transmitted through centuries. The
reader opens a curated argumentation room.

**Compositional behavior.** Single column (640 px), italic-400
title with the 36 px centred hairline rule (the manuscript
paragraph break), prose paragraphs at 0.92 em / 1.74 line-
height. Argument flows.

**Manuscript posture.** Archival argument. The reader hears
an inheritance speaking — Augustine confronting the question
of pre-creation time, Aquinas analyzing simultaneous vs
successive days. The interpretation arrives across centuries
and sits at the verse it addresses.

**Maturity.** ✓ Constitutionalized (v74). Stable.

### Linguistic witnesses — *philological gloss*

The marginal lexical note: bĕrēʾšît, tōhû wā-bōhû, naʿăśeh
ʾāḏām, hineni, ram-thicket, yhwh-jireh, el-roi, righteousness-
credited.

**Artifact logic.** A scholar's marginal annotation on a
single original-language word. The reader opens a tightly-
focused lexical witness.

**Compositional behavior.** Narrower column (560 px), roman
title (the transliterated lexeme as the foreground), 0.88 em
/ 1.66 line-height, dense paragraph rhythm (0.5 em spacers).
12.16 px verse-ref (lexicon-shorthand).

**Manuscript posture.** Gloss. Compact, lexeme-foregrounded,
philological. The reader senses a scholarly hand attending to
one word.

**Maturity.** ✓ Constitutionalized (v74). Stable.

### Architecture witnesses — *sacred-site inscription*

The patriarchal land-memory: altars, wells, pillars, caves,
mountains. Shechem, Bethel-altar-return, Beersheba, Machpelah,
Mount Moriah, wells of Isaac, the Bethel pillar, the cosmic
temple at Gen 1:7.

**Artifact logic.** A stone inscription preserving the
memory of a sacred place. The reader opens a quiet
monumental fragment.

**Compositional behavior.** Narrowest column (480 px),
small-caps italic title (inscriptional voice), engraved-
attribution verse-ref (letter-spacing 0.04 em), 0.90 em /
1.60 line-height (the stone holds).

**Manuscript posture.** Inscription. Site-named, brief,
sealed. The reader senses land-permanence — the witness has
been there longer than the codex.

**Maturity.** ✓ Constitutionalized (v74). Stable.

### Manuscript witnesses — *textual-evidence apparatus*

The critical-apparatus tradition: variants, vocalization,
manuscript traditions, Masoretic / LXX / Vulgate / Samaritan.
Currently 1 record (gen.1.1 manuscript witnesses).

**Artifact logic.** A page of textual apparatus — variants
collated, traditions catalogued. The reader opens a critical-
edition footnote.

**Compositional behavior.** Smallest column (500 px),
smallest font (0.84 em), tiniest verse-ref (0.70 em), roman
letter-spaced title (apparatus-heading register).

**Manuscript posture.** Apparatus. Dense, precise, footnote-
shaped. The reader senses textual-evidence collation — the
witness IS the transmission.

**Maturity.** ✓ Constitutionalized (v74). Stable.

### Ritual witnesses — *lived liturgical leaf*

The blessing, the festival, the daily practice. Currently
2 records: gen.1.3 ma'ariv ʿarāḇîm evening blessing (non-
chamber); gen.2.1 sabbath-pattern (AO·004 chamber).

**Artifact logic.** A liturgical leaf preserving spoken
practice. The reader opens a prayer that is still recited.

**Compositional behavior.** 560 px column, lighter italic-400
title (prayer doesn't claim authority), widest line-height in
the codex (1.78 — prayer-breath cadence).

**Manuscript posture.** Lived. Carried through generations
by enactment, not only preservation. The reader senses
practice rather than text.

**Maturity.** ✓ Constitutionalized (v74). Stable. v80
"carried through generations" transmission rubric reinforces.

### Reception-history witnesses — *layered chronological reading*

The accumulated reading across traditions: rûaḥ ʾĕlōhîm
across Christian/Jewish/modern; Imago Dei reception across
2000 years; three visitors at Mamre across Trinity icons;
the Akedah in Jewish, Christian, Islamic traditions.

**Artifact logic.** A page recording how this verse has been
read across centuries. The reader opens a chronological
sediment.

**Compositional behavior.** 600 px column (between commentary
and linguistic), 1.0 em spacers (wider — each tradition is
its own era).

**Manuscript posture.** Layered. The reader senses
chronological accumulation — Patristic, Rabbinic, Islamic,
modern, all in turn.

**Maturity.** ✓ Constitutionalized (v74). Stable. v80
"layered across centuries" transmission rubric reinforces.

### Cross-reference witnesses — *canonical-resonance column*

The list of other scripture passages a verse touches.
Currently 1 record: NT echoes of Gen 1:1 (John 1, Hebrews
11:3, Colossians 1:15-17).

**Artifact logic.** A marginal column of canonical echoes.
Scripture reverberating through scripture.

**Compositional behavior.** 540 px column, tightest line-
height (1.54), narrow spacers (0.45 em), and (v81) a dotted
left-edge hairline — the canonical-resonance axis. Each echo
hangs off the axis like a marginal note.

**Manuscript posture.** Echo column. Sparse, list-shaped,
vertical. The reader senses resonance.

**Maturity.** ✓ Constitutionalized (v74 + v81). Stable.

### Cosmology witnesses — *manuscript cosmogram*

The world-model: ANE cosmography, light-before-luminaries.
Currently 2 non-chamber records (gen.1.1, gen.1.3) + the
AO·001 firmament chamber.

**Artifact logic.** A manuscript-cosmographic encounter. A
preserved diagrammatic explication of the world this verse
assumes.

**Compositional behavior.** 620 px column (the widest of the
new five), 0.91 em / 1.70 line-height. **v83 cosmogram move
(this pass):** spacers between paragraphs carry a tiny
centered hairline mark — the manuscript-cosmogram visual
register. Spacer height pushed from 1.2 em to 1.4 em.
The body reads as a vertically-stacked cosmogram of cosmic
strata separated by horizontal rules.

**Manuscript posture.** Cosmogram. The reader's eye descends
through the cosmic layers (above firmament / firmament /
earth-disc / below earth) separated by hairline bands. Not
an essay; not an infographic; a manuscript explication of
the world-picture.

**Maturity.** ◐ Now formalized at the artifact level (v83).
Further deepening (per-stratum visual treatment) reserved.

### Doré plates — *monumental engraved interpretation*

19th-century French biblical illustration. 5 plates in
Genesis 1-11 (creation, expulsion, Cain/Abel, deluge,
Babel).

**Artifact logic.** A monumental visual interpretation. The
plate IS the witness; the engraving carries its own gravity.

**Compositional behavior.** Bespoke `_renderPlate` — no
parchment leaf, no provenance header; the engraving sits in
the dark chamber with a compact museum plaque beneath
(title · verse · artist · year · source). Cathedral entry
(v78: 1.2 s + 0.32 s held breath; the chamber's surrounding
warm-vignette deepens via `:has()`).

**Manuscript posture.** Monumental. The reader senses
cathedral arrival.

**Maturity.** ✓ Calibration mark (v73 + v78). Stable.

### Genealogy witnesses — *descent register / lineage architecture*

The patriarchal lineage chains: cain-seth, shem-to-terah,
terah-abram, lot-moab-ammon, isaac-ishmael, nahor-house,
jacob-esau, jacob-sons-by-mother, esau-edom, ephraim-
manasseh-adopted, sons-of-noah, antediluvian-line (AO·003
chamber).

**Artifact logic.** A descent register — names by generation,
indented by depth, marking dynastic continuity.

**Compositional behavior.** Tree-prefix indentation via
`_renderGenealogyVertical` (padding-left proportional to
prefix depth). 0.95 em / 1.75 line-height / opacity 0.86.
**v83 lineage move (this pass):** generation-transition rows
(detected via the existing `data-generation="transition"`
attribute) carry slight extra margin-top — the dynastic
descent reads as architecturally banded by generation.

**Manuscript posture.** Lineage architecture. The reader's
eye descends through the family tree; each generation a
band, the dynastic axis visible.

**Maturity.** ◐ Now formalized at the artifact level (v83).
Further architectural treatment (per-generation rubrics,
covenantal continuity marks) reserved.

### Atlas Object chambers — *bespoke encounter architecture*

Twelve realized chambers, each its own world: cosmology,
translation, lineage, sabbath, mountain, covenant, wisdom,
revelation, lament, incarnation, resurrection, sanctuary.

**Artifact logic.** Each chamber is its own preserved
intellectual artifact, with bespoke compositional
architecture: vocabulary tables, concentric enclosures,
layer stacks, rupture-then-return pairs, descent registers,
threshold rubrics, etc.

**Compositional behavior.** Twelve bespoke renderers. Each
chamber-leaf carries its own class (`.cosmology-chamber`,
`.sanctuary-chamber`, etc.) and its own compositional logic.

**Manuscript posture.** Realized encounter. The reader
enters; the chamber is the artifact.

**Maturity.** ✓ Twelve calibration marks (constitutional).
Stable.

### Map / itinerary witnesses — *manuscript cartography*

Currently 2 records: ANE constellations (gen.1.14), the
patriarchal journey map (gen.12.5).

**Artifact logic.** A textual cartography — a journey
recorded as itinerary prose, not as a rendered map.

**Maturity.** ◐ Unformalized (< 5 records). Deferred. Will
crystallize when more map records exist.

### Reconstruction / artifact / audio / timeline witnesses

Single-record families each. Reconstruction (Priestly source),
artifact (Tannînim), audio (cantillation of 1:31), timeline
(calendar-establishing verse).

**Artifact logic.** Each implies a distinct preserved-witness
form, but with only one record each, the family identity
cannot yet crystallize.

**Maturity.** ◐ Unformalized. Deferred per the constitution.

new JS. The two moves leverage existing structural attributes
(`.folio-body-spacer`, `data-generation="transition"`) that
have been in place since the early renderer work.

- **Phase 8 (Emotional Memory)** — emergent property. Each

## Posture

The codex stops aggressively iterating its infrastructure.
The infrastructure (v76-v82) becomes quiet constitutional
law. The frontier moves to the artifacts themselves — what
the reader encounters when they open a witness.

Cosmology, the under-realized family the brief named as
highest priority, graduates into manuscript-cosmogram form.
Genealogy, the latent-potential family, begins reading as
lineage architecture.

Each remaining mature family already has its constitutional
artifact identity (commentary's archival argument, linguistic's
gloss, architecture's inscription, manuscript's apparatus,
ritual's lived leaf, reception-history's chronology, cross-
reference's echo column, Doré's monumental plate).

The reader is no longer encountering "objects in a styled
codex." The reader is encountering preserved intellectual
artifacts — each in its own species — through a codex that
has become quiet enough to disappear behind them.
