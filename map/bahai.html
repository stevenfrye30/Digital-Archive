5 of 30 green — the green islands are E.G. Browne’s Victorian academic works (d.1926) and the original Arabic & Persian — the entire authorized English canon is © / free-to-read-but-not-PD (Shoghi Effendi d.1957 → 2028+).

The exact inverse of Zoroastrian — the newest major religion is the least public-domain. The original Arabic and Persian texts are all public-domain (their authors died 1850–1921), but the authoritative English canon is a 20th-century translation corpus made and actively copyright-controlled by Shoghi Effendi (d.1957 → not worldwide-PD until 2028+) and the Bahá’í World Centre. The official Bahá’í Reference Library hosts the whole canon free to read — but free-to-read is not public-domain (the 84000 model). The only clean green in English is E.G. Browne’s Victorian scholarship (d.1926).

The Báb 1819–1850 · the Forerunner5 texts

Siyyid ʻAlí Muhammad, the Báb, who heralded "Him Whom God shall make manifest." His major books have no authorized English translation; the only public-domain access is through Browne and Nicolas.
