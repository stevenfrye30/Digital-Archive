A tiny green core over a mostly-untranslated canon. The Śvetāmbara canon holds 45 Āgamas — but worldwide-public-domain English reaches only about five texts: Hermann Jacobi’s four Jaina Sūtras (SBE 22 & 45) and J.L. Jaini’s Tattvārtha Sūtra (1920). The other ~41 Āgamas, the entire Digambara canon, and the great later masters (Kundakunda, Hemacandra) are untranslated or under modern ©. So Jainism, like the Chinese Buddhist canon, is a small bright core over a dark, unread expanse.

The Śvetāmbara Āgamas 45 texts · 6 categories10 texts

The canon of the image-worshipping Śvetāmbaras: 11 Aṅgas (of an original 12 — the Dṛṣṭivāda is lost), 12 Upāṅgas, 10 Prakīrṇakas, 6 Chedasūtras, 4 Mūlasūtras, 2 Cūlikāsūtras. (The Sthānakavāsī and Terāpanthī accept only 32.)
