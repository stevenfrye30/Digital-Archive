The Map of Hindu Texts

The eight zones

    The pattern the colours reveal: the public-domain frontier follows the calendar of scholarship, not the age of the texts. Green dominates the śruti core and inner rings — the 19th- and early-20th-century Indologists (Müller, Griffith, Keith, Wilson, Thibaut, Ganguli, Woodroffe, Pope) translated the Vedas, Darśanas and epics long ago. Amber takes over at the Purāṇas and the branches, translated mostly after 1970 and still in copyright. The edge of what a public archive can freely hold isn't the edge of the tradition — it's roughly the year 1930.
