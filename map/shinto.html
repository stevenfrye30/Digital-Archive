3 of 13 green — the mythic core (Kojiki, Nihongi) is fully public-domain through Chamberlain and Aston; the liturgy (Norito, Engishiki) and later theology stay amber or red.

A green mythic core, a red liturgy. Shintō has no single scripture — but its two great foundation-stones, the Kojiki (712) and the Nihon Shoki (720), were translated into public domain by Chamberlain (1882) and Aston (1896), so the whole mythology of the kami is freely readable. Past that, the map darkens: the Norito ritual prayers and the Engishiki have no clean public-domain English (only Florenz’s German or copyrighted Philippi), the great Man’yōshū anthology is ©, and the later theology stays red.
