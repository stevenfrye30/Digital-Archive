19 of 30 green — most new religions are already public-domain because their founders wrote in English and died before 1955 (Joseph Smith, Mary Baker Eddy, Ellen White, Blavatsky, Kardec) — the amber is corporate copyright (the New World Translation, Religious Science) or a missing English translation of an old source (Tenrikyo, Cao Đài); the red is the enforced 20th-c canons (Scientology, Wicca, Unification).

A new faith is not a closed book. Unlike the ancient scriptures, these were written within living memory — yet most are already public-domain, because their founders wrote in English and died before 1955: the Book of Mormon (1830), Science and Health (1875), Ellen White, Blavatsky, Kardec. The line falls almost exactly at the institutional twentieth century: the amber and red belong to movements whose scripture is corporately controlled (the New World Translation, Scientology, the Divine Principle) — or, for the Japanese and Vietnamese new religions (Tenrikyo, Cao Đài), whose source text is old and public-domain but whose only English translation is a modern copyright. This is the mirror of the Bahá’í door, whose authorized translator died in 1957 and left it nearly all amber. Rastafari lives on the Indigenous door; the Ahmadiyya reform of Islam on the Abrahamic door.

A structural atlas of modern & new religious movements — coloured by worldwide public-domain English availability. The green front = 19th-c founders who wrote in English and died pre-1955 (Smith d.1844, Eddy d.1910, White d.1915, Russell d.1916, Blavatsky d.1891, Kardec d.1869/Blackwell tr. 1875). Amber = corporate/institutional © (New World Translation, Holmes d.1960, Urantia) OR an old PD source with only a modern © English translation (Tenrikyo, Oomoto, Konkokyo, Cao Đài — "translation-blocked, not text-blocked"). Red = enforced 20th-c canons (Scientology, Wicca, Nation of Islam, Unification).
