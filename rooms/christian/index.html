The mirror image of Buddhism. There the core was free and the frontier was the untranslated ocean. Here the scriptural core is the greenest we’ve mapped — the whole Bible and the whole Qurʾān are public-domain, and Christianity’s 38-volume patristic shelf makes it the greenest pillar in the archive. But two different walls rise past the core: Judaism’s rabbinic layer goes amber (Danby’s Mishnah is US-copyright until 2029; the standard Talmud is © or CC-non-commercial), and Islam’s hinterland goes red — every Hadith collection, the Sīra, and the tafsīrs exist in English only under modern ©.
