The Map of Zoroastrian Texts

The scripture of the fire-tending faith of ancient Persia — the Avesta of Zarathustra and the Pahlavi books of its later priests. Coloured by whether a worldwide-public-domain English translation exists.

The purest Sacred-Books-of-the-East door in the archive. Almost the entire canon was translated between 1880 and 1887 by three scholars — Darmesteter (the Vendidād and Yašts), Mills (the Yasna, Visperad and Gāthās), and E.W. West (the Pahlavi books) — all long in the public domain. So Zoroastrianism, though one of the world’s oldest revealed religions, is also one of the greenest: nearly every text is freely readable. Only the modern verse retranslations of the Gāthās and the late Persian Rivāyats stay amber.
