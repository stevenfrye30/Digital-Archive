Rooms of the world's sacred-text traditions. Six rooms are open — Hinduism, Buddhism, the Abrahamic room (Judaism, Christianity, and Islam together), the East Asian room (Confucianism and Daoism), the Zoroastrian room (the Avesta and the Pahlavi books), the Ancient World room (Egypt, Mesopotamia, and the old mythologies), the Gnostic room (gnosis and the dualist stream), and the Jain room (the Āgamas and the masters). Every other tradition stands mapped: its full territory — holdings, possibilities, and honest gaps — is open in the Map of Sacred Texts while its immersive room is built. These rooms map religious structure; the archive holds a curated, incomplete selection of readable texts and does not claim complete canons. Enter a room, then use your browser's Back to return to the Hall — or the room's own "Return to the Hall" link. (Texts still open in the reader in a separate tab.)
